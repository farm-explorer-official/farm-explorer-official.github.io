Privacy Policy

Farm Explorer

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in connection with the mobile application “Farm Explorer”.

1. Data Controller

Farm Explorer / Hacks
Email: farm.explorer.official@gmail.com

2. General Information

Farm Explorer is a privately developed, free hobby game. The current version of the app does not use advertising, AdMob, in-app purchases, or user accounts.

To the best of our current knowledge, the app does not collect, store, or transmit users' personal data to the developer or to third parties engaged by the developer.

3. Personal Data

The app does not request personal information such as a name, postal address, telephone number, or email address from users.

No registration is required and no user account is needed to play the game.

4. Transmission of Data

The current version of Farm Explorer does not transmit personal data to the developer and does not use the developer's own servers to store user data.

In particular, personal data is not processed for advertising, analytics, marketing, or profiling purposes.

5. Local Game Data

The game may store game-related information, such as game progress or settings, locally on the user's device.

This information is not accessed by the developer and is not transmitted to a server operated by the developer.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of Farm Explorer does not use advertising services, analytics services, or other external services that collect personal user data on behalf of the developer.

7. Data Security

Because the current version does not transmit personal user data to the developer or process such data on the developer's own servers, the developer does not centrally store such personal data.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not store personal user data on its own systems.

Local game data may be removed by uninstalling the app or, where supported by the operating system, by deleting the app's stored data.

9. Rights of Data Subjects

Where personal data is processed by the data controller, data subjects have, subject to applicable legal requirements, rights including the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and objection to certain processing activities.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact the privacy contact listed above by email.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the app or the way data is processed changes. The current version will always be published on this webpage.
